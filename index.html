1.0The brand DNA

"Stay Ready" isn't a product slogan — it's a worldview. Built on four pillars from the field manual: Mindset · Conduct · Readiness · Character. Applied to everything from how you train to how you eat to how you show up.

Target audience: hardcore athletes, operators, and anyone who does hard work and isn't afraid of carbs and fats. The mindset is for everyone. The product is for the ones putting in the reps.

2.0Why this works

Every successful lifestyle brand we're learning from — Liquid Death, Liver King, KillCliff, Goggins — shares one trait: the founder is the brand, and the product is the artifact people buy to participate in the worldview.

FM 1-00.04 · Business Model

The Model

Three product lines, one brand. Each line has a job — only one carries the P&L.

PRIMARY · 70% OF NET
Bars

320 cal endurance bar · 15P / 31C / 15F. The recurring revenue engine.

  • Subscription compounds — this is the cheat code
  • $4.50 retail / $3.75 in subscription
  • $1.25 COGS · ~58–72% gross margin
  • 12-bar monthly box at $54
SECONDARY · 20% OF NET
Apparel

Next Level tri-blend. Paid marketing on people's backs. Not a profit center.

  • $12.65 all-in cost
  • Suggested retail: $30–35
  • Modest margin, reinvest in ads
  • Quarterly drops, limited runs
TERTIARY · 5% OF NET
Field Manual

The doctrine. Free with every order. Paid premium boxed set.

  • Free wallet cards = brand evangelism
  • $20 boxed set with full set of cards
  • Lead magnet for email capture
  • The cult formation tool

FM 1-00.05 · 365-Day Roadmap

The Path

Four phases. Four budgets. Four stage gates. If a gate misses, that's the trigger to pause and reassess before deploying more capital.

5.1Phase 1 — Foundations

Days 1–60 · $8K · Goal: legal, live, first 100 sales.

  • File the trademark this week — "STAY READY" word mark, Class 25 (apparel) + Class 29/30 (food)
  • Lock the kitchen — verify aunt's pastry shop has current health permit + ServSafe-certified manager
  • FDA-compliant labels — nutrition panel, ingredients, allergen statement, net weight, manufacturer info
  • Product liability insurance, food-specific — non-optional
  • Business bank account + accounting in QuickBooks
  • Shopify build — bars (single / 6-pack / sub), shirt, free FM card capture
  • Pricing reset — $4.50 retail / $3.75 sub. Current $3 leaves money on the table
  • June 21 event — bring 300 bars, 80 shirts, signage. Goal: 150 customers, 100 emails, 30 first subscribers

5.2Phase 2 — Validation

Days 61–180 · $10K · Goal: 150 active subscribers, content cadence locked.

  • Luie content: 5x/week minimum across IG + TikTok — workouts, philosophy clips, behind-the-scenes
  • Podcast tour: gym buddy first, then 8–12 fitness/veteran/lifestyle podcasts in 90 days
  • 1 in-person event/month — CrossFit comps, Spartan-adjacent, veteran charity events
  • Subscription launch (Day 75) — three tiers: 6-bar ($28), 12-bar ($54), 24-bar ($102)
  • Email/SMS automation — cart abandon, sub pause/skip flows
  • Wholesale conversations begin: 5 local boxes (CrossFit/F45), 2 specialty retailers
  • One MWR / unit point-of-contact via Luie's network — relationship building only

5.3Phase 3 — Scale

Days 181–300 · $5K · Goal: 215+ subs holding, production scaled.

  • Co-packer conversations — small-batch SoCal co-packers, target $1.50–1.75/bar at 5K-bar minimums
  • 2nd bar SKU launch — drives subscription retention by 20–30%
  • Premium field manual boxed set — $20 retail, ~$6 cost
  • Apparel drop #2 — new colorways, limited runs (200–400 units)
  • SOF/SEAL channel pursuit — first formal pitch to a unit or MWR program
  • Podcast / PR push to amplify momentum

5.4Phase 4 — Consolidate

Days 301–365 · $2K · Goal: hit $100K, build Year 2 thesis.

  • Q4 holiday push — gift bundles, "Stay Ready Year One" anniversary box
  • Year 2 thesis: wholesale activation, second product category (hydration? recovery shake?), or vertical content
  • Don't add complexity until $100K is locked

FM 1-00.07 · Risk Register

The Risks

Eight risks I'd plan around. Each has a hedge. The kill-switch ($25K loss) is the final hedge.

Trademark conflict with existing mark Medium High File immediately. Clearance search before any media spend.
Cottage food law gap — whey + refrigerated bar likely won't qualify in CA High High Aunt's licensed kitchen is the answer. Don't sell DTC until kitchen + label compliant.
Founder content cadence drops off Medium Critical 30-day content bank in Phase 1. Bring in part-time content assistant Phase 2.
Subscription churn > 10%/month Medium High 2nd SKU by Day 200. Pause-don't-cancel flow. Surprise FM drops in boxes.
Apparel dead-stock on a drop Low–Med Medium Pre-orders for drops 2+. Conservative batch sizes. Color rotation.
Single-founder dependency — injury, burnout High Critical SOPs documented Day 1. Personal disability insurance. Wife trained on ops basics.
Liability event from food product Low Catastrophic Product liability insurance Day 1. Allergen labeling. Lot tracking from first batch.
Capital exhaustion before subscription compounds Medium Critical Stage gates at Day 60, 120, 180. Cut spend if behind. Kill at $25K loss.
